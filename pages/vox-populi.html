Vox Populi - Keep Up With The World: Your news commonplace.


Vox Populi is an all-in-one, quick and customizable feed for your media outlets, take control back and tune everything your news to the liking.


TRIVIA: This app started as an away for me to read hacker news on my phone, but with time it turned into a full RSS client with a text-embedding-based recommendation system (thanks to TensorFlow's MediaPipe). Even though I'm probably gonna be it's only user, I still hope this app can help people catch up with the chaos that is the modern day.
